CogniDir

·Main·2026

Research area: Robust misinformation detection

How can fake news detectors adapt to different mechanisms of LLM-generated malicious comments?

CogniDir strengthens comment-aware fake news detection through mechanism-specific malicious comment generation and feedback-driven training mixtures. InfoDirichlet Resampling adapts exposure across attack groups on Weibo16, Weibo20, and RumourEval-19.

CoT is Not the Chain of Truth

·ICML·2026

Research area: Reasoning-model safety

How can reasoning-trace safety be evaluated and localized inside language models?

We study unsafe content in generated reasoning traces, localize safety-related layers and attention heads with Jacobian spectral analysis, and evaluate targeted safety interventions on fake-news generation and HarmBench.
