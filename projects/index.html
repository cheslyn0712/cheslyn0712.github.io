CogniDir

·EMNLP·2026

LLM-generated comments can attack comment-based fake news detectors through fact distortion, logical confusion, and emotional manipulation. CogniDir scores each attack group's vulnerability and reallocates the next epoch's comment mixture with the deterministic Dirichlet mean. On the reported Weibo20 robust-training comparison, Macro-F1 moves from 0.803 (ARG) to 0.947, a relative gain of about 17.9% over that baseline.

CoT is Not the Chain of Truth

·ICML·2026

The paper studies generated reasoning traces, using fake-news generation as the main setting and HarmBench as a further check. A final refusal does not by itself show that the trace was safe. The analysis localizes divergence to layers and attention heads with Jacobian spectral measurements, perturbations, and a reported critical-head intervention. The joint Unsafe plus Potential Unsafe rate in the generation plots is not the conditional rate among refusals.
